Delivery, returns and FAQ

Everything you need to know before you order.

How ordering works

  • Add your favourites to your bag and send your order on WhatsApp.
  • We confirm availability, your delivery fee and how to pay by M-Pesa.
  • Your order is wrapped in plum tissue and sent to you.

Draft wording. We will confirm every detail with you before launch.

Delivery

We deliver in Nairobi and across Kenya. Nairobi orders usually arrive within one to two working days. Other areas depend on the courier, and we will tell you the timing and cost before you pay.

Returns

If something arrives damaged or is not right, message us within seven days and we will make it right with a replacement or refund.

Questions

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa after we confirm your order on WhatsApp. Card payments will be added later.

Can I send a gift to someone else?

Yes. Tell us the delivery details and your note, and we will wrap it and add your message.

Do you ship outside Nairobi?

Yes, across Kenya through trusted couriers.
