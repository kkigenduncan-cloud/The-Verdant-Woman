Questions, answered

Who is coaching for?

Any woman who feels ready for more: more clarity, more confidence, more peace. No experience needed.

How are sessions held?

Online by video, with in-person options in Nairobi for retreats. Final formats will be confirmed at launch.

Is it faith-based?

Sessions are rooted in faith and open to every woman. You bring your story, we bring the structure.
